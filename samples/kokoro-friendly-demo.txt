HOST: Create a project, import this script, assign two voices, then hit Generate all.
GUEST: After that, play the full mix, tweak one paragraph, and generate that line again.
HOST: For tech words, the built-in glossary helps. Say C I C D, or JSON, and Kokoro will usually handle it.
GUEST: You can also force pronunciation. Write Docker [as: docker] if a name ever sounds wrong.
HOST: Keep paragraphs to one or two sentences. Long walls of text are harder to listen to.
GUEST: And leave a little air between ideas. Short turns make the stitch feel like a show.
HOST: Export when you like a take. You get a full mix W A V, or a zip of each paragraph.
GUEST: That is the loop. Write, generate, listen, revise, export.
